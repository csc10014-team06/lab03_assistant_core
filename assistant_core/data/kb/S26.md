# S26 — Student services (student handbook 2026)

Each `## <id> <title>` heading starts one chunk.

## S26#1 Borrowing library books
Students may borrow up to 5 books from the Library at the same time. The loan period is 14 days and can be renewed once on the library website.

## S26#2 Overdue books
Overdue books are charged 2,000 VND per book per day. Students with unpaid library fines cannot borrow new books.

## S26#3 Lost student card
A lost or damaged student card must be reported to Student Affairs (room I.102). A replacement card costs 50,000 VND and is ready after 5 working days.

## S26#4 University email password
Every student gets a university email account. If you forget the password, bring your student card to the IT Helpdesk (room E.005) to reset it.

## S26#5 Campus wifi
To connect to the campus wifi network, log in with your student ID and portal password.

## S26#6 Merit scholarship
A merit scholarship is awarded each semester to students with a GPA of at least 3.2 and a conduct score of at least 80. No application is needed.

## S26#7 Dormitory
Students apply for the university dormitory in July each year. Priority goes to first-year students from other provinces.

## S26#8 Health insurance
Student health insurance is mandatory and costs 1,263,000 VND per year. It is paid together with the first-semester tuition.
