# H26 — Academic regulations (student handbook 2026)

Each `## <id> <title>` heading starts one chunk. An optional `Alias:` line links the
chunk to the matching rule id in `handbook.json`, so the rules-only path can be scored
against the same expected sources.

## H26#1 Retaking a course
Alias: H26-4.2
Students may retake a course they failed. Retake credits are charged at 1.5 times the normal rate. Final-year students must register a retake before the week 2 deadline.

## H26#2 Thesis registration
Alias: H26-5.3
Students may register a thesis when they have at least 120 accumulated credits and a GPA of at least 2.0.

## H26#3 Late thesis registration
Alias: H26-5.4
Late thesis registration requires approval from the Faculty and the supervisor.

## H26#4 Credit load
Alias: H26-6.1
A regular semester load is between 14 and 30 credits. Registering more than 30 credits requires approval from the Faculty.

## H26#5 Exam absence
Alias: H26-7.2
Students absent from a final exam for medical reasons must submit evidence to the Training Office within 3 working days.

## H26#6 Grade review
Students who disagree with a final exam grade may request a regrade from their Faculty within 7 days after grades are published. A regrade costs 50,000 VND per course.

## H26#7 Leave of absence
Students may suspend their studies for up to 2 semesters for health, family or financial reasons. The request form goes to the Training Office before the semester starts.

## H26#8 Course withdrawal and refund
Students who withdraw from a course before week 3 receive a 70% tuition refund. After week 3 no tuition is refunded.

## H26#9 Academic warning
A student whose semester GPA is below 1.0 receives an academic warning. Three consecutive warnings lead to dismissal.

## H26#10 Graduation requirements
To graduate, students need 138 accumulated credits, a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 and an English certificate at level B1 or above.
